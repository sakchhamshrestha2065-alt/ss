My First Project

This is my very first project, and I am proud of the effort and learning that went into making it. It represents my starting point and the first step in building my web design skills.

Portfolio Website

I created this personal portfolio to introduce myself, share my goals, and show my very first web project in a clean and simple layout.

This project includes my home page, about section, goals, contact page, and a project page that explains what I made and what I learned while creating it. I wanted the website to feel personal, organized, and easy to explore.

I used HTML and CSS to design the pages, arrange the content, and make the overall look clean and attractive. Even though this is my first project, I tried my best to make it thoughtful, creative, and meaningful.

Through this project, I learned how to structure a webpage, style different sections, and bring together the content in a way that looks professional and reflects my personality.
